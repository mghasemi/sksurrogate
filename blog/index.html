    An Honest Benchmark of My Own AutoML Package

    At equal evaluation budgets, surrogate-guided search does not beat Optuna-TPE on accuracy. What is real: the response surface matters more than the search mechanism, and the most useful finding was a defect in our own pipeline — ordinal encoding was silently masking the surrogate's advantage.
