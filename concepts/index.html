Concepts

Three ideas do the work in SKSurrogate. Each is defined here in the terms the documentation uses, with the evidence the package publishes on its own behaviour.

optimization

Surrogate-based optimization

Fit an approximate model to expensive evaluations and use it to choose the next point — when that saves evaluations, and when it does not.
uncertainty

Split-conformal intervals

Finite-sample marginal coverage from a calibration split, and the assumptions that make the guarantee fail.
evaluation

Nested cross-validation

Keep model selection separate from the estimate of generalisation, so the score you report is not the score you optimised.
